Knock-up status card - 3 concepts

Three different ideas, not three colours. In all of them the card and the body share the same parabola of 1.3 s (apex at 0.65 s, no hover), the card itself moves and rotates during the flight and settles upright, and the landing closes with a small shake and a dust ring (D-1205). No white screens, no strobe (D-418). Each option loops and has a Repeat button.

Card concepts

Same card (name, HP/MP bars, red seal), same flight, same landing. Only the idea behind the motion changes.

Concept 1

Thrown slab

The block is launched flat and rips a banded motion ribbon behind it; three afterimage silhouettes mark where it just was. One full bullet spin, landing upright.

Concept 2

Shatter seal

The red seal cracks the card open: three shards drift apart while airborne, the crack line stays lit, and the block seals shut on impact with the ground.

Concept 3

Jelly slice

The card deforms like a sheet: it stretches along the flight, its horizontal slices shear at peak speed, a lime band wipes up the face, and it snaps flat on landing.
